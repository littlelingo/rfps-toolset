Rules for this file: a discriminator is something a competitor cannot honestly claim; proof is a register ID (E-nnn) or the word ASSERTION; the benefit is written in the agency's own words (their risk, cost, schedule, staff load); competitor notes are assumptions and never appear in the response text.
